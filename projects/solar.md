As part of my Public Interest Technology club's partnership with the Boston Food Forest Coalition, I helped to design a modular solar system to power drip irrigation for public food gardens and created ways for the community to engage and learn from the project. I helped choose components for the system, provided paths for mounting and assembly, and made a lot of documentation and graphics for people visiting the garden to engage with the solar system.
