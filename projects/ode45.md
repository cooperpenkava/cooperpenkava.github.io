For my Applied Mathematics course, I recreated MATLAB's ode45 function using the Runge-Kutta method. We started by building the basic euler's method step function, then evolved it slowly, adding in more concepts (RK4, error estimation/control, adaptive step size, etc.) until we had our own version of ode45. We used it to simulate dynamic systems, building our own representations of the wave equation and more.
