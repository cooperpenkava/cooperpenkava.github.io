For my Principles of Integrated Engineering Course final project, I worked with three other engineers to create a moving basketball hoop that sunk a basket every time. We had seven weeks to accomplish our goal, split up into three sprints, and by the time everything was said and done, we had a system that moves the basketball hoop in real time, adjusting to the ball's trajectory to ensure (almost!) every shot goes in.

I personally was very interested in the physics of the system and worked with everyone on the team to lead every aspect towards the goal. This meant that I worked with Andrew, our software person, on the physics of the ball and how to find it's end location from its previous known locations, along with the equations deriving the amount of cable to let out for this predicted position. I worked with Agatha, our electrical person, to build the firmware defining how our motors should bring in/let out cable based on our software's location and cable amounts. And then Minu and I worked on everything mechanical. Of that, I was created the tensioners, which ensured tension even as we quickly let out wire, and built the frame from scrap wood and 3D printed gussets. It was one of the most fun and rewarding projects I've ever worked on!
