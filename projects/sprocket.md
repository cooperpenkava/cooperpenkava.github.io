_**Skills Used:**_
 - _**Mechanical Design - Chain and Sprocket**_
 - _**Theories of Failure - Chain and Sprocket**_
 - _**Solidworks Linear FEA - Static, Modal, & Fatigue**_
 - _**Design Parameterization/Geometric Optimization**_
 - _**Excel Calculators**_

For my Formula SAE team, I lightweighted a main drivetrain sprocket while ensuring its safety through hand calculations and FEA simulation. I first researched the forces and stresses present in chain and sprocket systems, focusing on how each tooth takes part of the chain force. I then brought this into theories of failure for chains and sprockets, doing hand calculations (bolt tension needs, bearing stress, etc.) for the connection points between the chain, sprocket, and the rest of the system. Additionally, I did Static, Modal, and Fatigue FEA to ensure my hand calcs made sense, to ensure that potential lightweighting patterns didn't have resonant frequencies within our operating speeds, and to ensure that small stresses did not result in failure by the end of a race. I then integrated all of these studies into geometrically parameterized studies, where I could test how far we could take the lightweighting pattern without failing, attempting to minimize mass while ensuring part strength (FoS > 1.5).
