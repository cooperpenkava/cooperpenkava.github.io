As part of my dual enrollment program with a local community college, I designed and created a fully 3D printable pendulum clock as my engineering capstone project. It was my first big engineering project. Looking back, there's so many ways I could improve the design and so many ways I could have saved a lot of headaches, but experience is a great teacher. I modelled the whole thing in Solidworks, ensured the gear train ran with the correct timing, and split it all into components that we could 3D print.
